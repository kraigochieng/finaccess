Where the niches are

One niche per product, chosen by a fixed rule: the segment with the most adults short of the national share, with the reasons non-users in that segment give for staying out. The survey does not ask why for digital credit, SACCOs or pensions.

Segments overlap (the same 18-25 year olds appear in several niches), so the sizes describe each niche on its own and should not be added together.

Limits

  • Descriptive, not causal. Young adults lag on loans and insurance partly because of life stage, so a gap is not automatically an opportunity.
  • Weights are applied, but survey strata and clusters are not, so there are no design-based standard errors. County estimates rest on roughly 350-550 interviews each.
  • The public data has no urban/rural variable, and this is one survey round, so no trends.
  • "Served" uses the survey's own derived indicators. Digital credit is shown two ways: broad (Hustler Fund, mobile money and mobile banking loans) and app-based lenders only, which the report also puts near 2%.
  • The survey measures usage, not willingness to pay. Market size is not revenue.
